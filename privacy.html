Privacy Policy

Scrap Orc Survivor · Last updated: November 4, 2026

Data the game keeps

Scrap Orc Survivor stores your progress (coins, upgrades, gear, saved runs, run statistics, settings and crash logs) only on your device. The game has no accounts, and none of this data is sent to us or anyone else.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which you can choose to watch for in-game rewards. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is governed by Google's privacy policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Your consent

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form when it first starts, before any ads are loaded. You can accept or decline personalized ads there; if you decline, AdMob may still show non-personalized ads, which use only limited data such as your general location from your IP address.

You can change your choice at any time: tap the sun in the game's main menu and choose Privacy to open the consent form again. The button shows wherever the consent form applies.

Ads are always optional. If you never tap an ad button, no ad is shown.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
